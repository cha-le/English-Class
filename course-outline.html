Elementary Program

小学英语班 · Ages 6–12
Weeks 1–2

Foundations & Greetings

  • Alphabet, phonics, and basic sounds
  • Introductions and everyday greetings
  • Numbers, colours, and simple nouns
Weeks 3–4

My World — Family & Home

  • Family members and relationships
  • Describing where you live
  • Simple present tense practice
Weeks 5–6

School & Daily Routines

  • Classroom vocabulary and commands
  • Talking about school subjects
  • Time expressions and daily schedules
Weeks 7–8

Animals, Nature & the World

  • Animal names and habitats
  • Describing size, colour, and behaviour
  • Simple reading passages
Weeks 9–10

Food, Shopping & Places

  • Food vocabulary and mealtimes
  • Ordering and shopping dialogues
  • Asking for and giving directions
Weeks 11–12

Stories & Creative Writing

  • Reading and retelling short stories
  • Writing simple sentences and captions
  • Speaking confidence activities
🏫

High School Program

高中英语班 · Ages 13–18
Weeks 1–2

Grammar Deep Dive

  • Tenses: past, present, future, and perfect
  • Conditionals and modal verbs
  • Common grammar mistakes and how to fix them
Weeks 3–4

Academic Reading

  • Skimming, scanning, and close reading
  • Identifying main ideas and supporting details
  • Vocabulary in context strategies
Weeks 5–6

Essay Writing Skills

  • Paragraph structure: topic, evidence, analysis
  • Opinion, argumentative, and descriptive essays
  • Introduction and conclusion techniques
Weeks 7–8

Speaking & Debate

  • Presenting ideas clearly and confidently
  • Discussion strategies and turn-taking
  • Mock debate and opinion exercises
Weeks 9–10

Listening & Comprehension

  • Note-taking from spoken English
  • Accent awareness and natural speech patterns
  • Podcast and video-based exercises
Weeks 11–12

Exam Preparation

  • Practice papers and timed exercises
  • Common exam question types
  • Review, feedback, and personalised study plan

This is a sample outline. Every student's program is tailored to their level and goals. Get in touch on WeChat to discuss what's right for your child.
