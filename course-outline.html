About the Course

CAVoice Cambridge KET Speaking Bootcamp is a course focused on raising KET speaking ability, taught by outstanding Chinese-Canadian high school and university students who grew up in Canada. The course has 15 lessons of 1 hour each and is built around the core topics of the official KET syllabus, following the two KET Speaking task types: Part 1 (individual questions) and Part 2 (pair / examiner interaction). Every student gets plenty of speaking practice and ongoing personalised guidance in class.

Course Goals

After completing the course, students will be able to:
✓

Master the common KET Speaking question types and answering techniques.

✓

Learn to answer naturally in the exam instead of reciting memorised answers.

✓

Give longer, more complete and more logical answers.

✓

Improve pronunciation, intonation and fluency.

✓

Reduce common Chinglish expressions and sound more natural.

✓

Build speaking confidence and the courage to share your own ideas.

⭐

Why Choose This Course?

🍁

Raised in Canada

Our tutors grew up in Canada and coach with native-speaker pronunciation and expressions. Real-life examples are woven into every class, so students learn authentic English, not memorised answers.

🌏

Bilingual & Bicultural

A Chinese-Canadian background means our tutors understand how Chinese students think and express themselves, and where the pain points in spoken English are, so they can target difficulties precisely.

🤝

Like an Older Sibling

With a small age gap, tutors feel like friendly older siblings who encourage kids to speak up, and who can explain English from the student's point of view, not just say "this is the right way."
