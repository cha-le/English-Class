About the Classes

All sessions are held live on Tencent Meeting (腾讯会议). I'll send you a link via WeChat before every class. It only takes a minute to set up.
Sessions run Monday to Friday and are scheduled in your child's local timezone. We'll agree on a fixed daily time when you sign up.
Sessions are kept small so every student gets plenty of speaking time and personal attention. Message me on WeChat to ask about current class sizes.
Each session is 60 minutes, following the Warm-up → Core Lesson → Group Practice → Presentation structure used in Canadian classrooms.

Enrolment & Level

The Beginner program suits ages 9–12 with foundational English. The Advanced program suits ages 13–16 with some speaking experience. I'll assess your child's level at the start and place them in the right class.
Just add me on WeChat and send a message with your child's age, grade, and current English level. I'll reply quickly with next steps.

Payment & Pricing
