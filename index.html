FAQ

Common Questions

Everything parents want to know before enrolling.

Classes are held online via Tencent Meeting (腾讯会议). Once you contact me on WeChat, I'll send you the meeting link and help you get set up — it only takes a minute!
Classes run Monday to Friday, scheduled in your child's local timezone so the times always work for your family. We'll agree on a consistent daily time when you sign up.
Payment details will be discussed over WeChat. I accept common methods convenient for Chinese families. For weekly and summer packages, discounts are applied automatically.
Life happens! Please message me on WeChat as early as possible if your child can't attend. We'll do our best to reschedule or find a make-up arrangement.
Classes are kept small and focused so every student gets plenty of attention and speaking time. Contact me on WeChat to find out current availability and class sizes.
All levels are welcome — from complete beginners to students preparing for advanced exams. I'll assess your child's level at the start and tailor lessons accordingly.
