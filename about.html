Teaching Approach

🗣️

Speaking-First

Every lesson centres on getting students to actually speak. I model natural English, then immediately put it into practice through pair work, discussion, and presentations.

🍁

Canadian Classroom Style

My classes simulate a real Canadian school environment — collaborative, encouraging, and focused on critical thinking. Students experience what it's actually like to learn in North America.

🔄

Listen → Speak → Discuss → Present

Every session follows a proven cycle: warm-up, core instruction, group practice, and a final presentation. No passive listening — students are active every minute.

💬

Personalised Feedback

I give detailed, constructive feedback to every student after each session — and keep parents updated via WeChat so you always know how your child is progressing.

💡

My Teaching Philosophy

Refuse rote learning. Embrace real communication.

Traditional English teaching in China often focuses on grammar rules, translation, and memorisation. My approach is different. I designed this program to feel like a Canadian classroom — where students are expected to think, share opinions, work in groups, and present ideas out loud.

The goal isn't just to pass an exam. It's to build the kind of English fluency and confidence that lasts — the ability to walk into any English-speaking room and hold your own.
