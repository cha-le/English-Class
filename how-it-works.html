🚀 Getting Started

How It Works

From your first message to your first class — here's exactly what to expect.

1

Add Charlie on WeChat

Send a message with your child's grade and current English level. I'll reply quickly to say hi and answer any questions.

2

Choose Your Plan

Pick between the Beginner or Advanced program. Sessions run Monday–Friday in your local timezone, so routines are easy to build.

3

Start Learning!

Join live sessions on Tencent Meeting, get personalised feedback, and watch your English improve week by week.

🗓️

What Happens After You Reach Out

1

Initial WeChat Message

Tell me your child's age, grade, and current English level. I usually reply within a few hours.

2

Level Check & Program Match

Based on age and ability, I'll recommend the Beginner (ages 9–12) or Advanced (ages 13–16) program.

3

Schedule Your Sessions

We'll agree on a consistent daily time, Monday to Friday, that works in your timezone.

4

Confirm Payment

Choose per-session, full-week, or summer package pricing. Payment is arranged over WeChat.

5

Join Your First Class

I'll send the Tencent Meeting link before your first session. Just click and join — no installation hassle!

💻

Where Classes Happen

💬

Tencent Meeting (腾讯会议)

All classes are held live on Tencent Meeting — a familiar, reliable platform for Chinese families. You'll receive a link via WeChat before every session, and I'll help with setup the first time if needed.
